Quantum correlations.
Network-level services.Precision starts
with connection.

I am a doctoral researcher at the Deutsche Telekom Chair of Communication Networks at TU Dresden. I study quantum synchronization and quantum-network services, combining analytical modelling, numerical simulation, and a practical view of communication infrastructure.

My research considers time-correlated photon detection, entangled-photon protocols, Hong–Ou–Mandel interference, and the timing characteristics of quantum emitters. I examine how source behaviour, detector imperfections, channel delay, and clock drift influence the services a network can deliver.

Before joining TU Dresden in 2022, I completed an Integrated M.Sc. at Visva-Bharati University, with dissertation research at CSIR–National Physical Laboratory. Research at Helmholtz Institute Jena and a visiting PhD appointment in the Wehner Group at QuTech, TU Delft, have shaped my interest in the connection between optical physics and network engineering.
